A little transparency

Your library. Your rules.

Open viewing

Movie Adda does not require a sign-in to browse, choose seasons, open streams, or save favorites. Streaming providers may have their own access requirements.

Only the upload workspace is locked

The passcode and unlocked state are not saved. Closing the uploader, reloading the page, or 10 minutes of inactivity ends the upload session.

Stored on this device

This standalone edition saves your title links, uploaded posters and watchlist in this browser, not on a server. Uploaded posters are resized on your device before being stored. Nothing syncs to other devices, and clearing browser data removes it all. If browser storage is unavailable, changes last only for this visit.

Your links, no demo videos

The collection starts empty. Only titles you add appear here. Posters you upload stay on this device; linked images and videos load from their original providers, which have their own privacy policies.

Before public deployment: this browser-only upload lock is a convenience gate, not secure server authentication. A genuinely private admin session and a shared, protected library require an authenticated backend.
